KinEMOS — tracker verification

The tracker's unit tests hand it synthetic images directly. This page closes the gap between that and the app: it encodes a real video of a plate moving along a known trajectory, reads it back through the actual frame server, converts frames through the actual trackerSourceFrom adapter, and tracks. So the codec's chroma subsampling, its quantisation, the canvas readback and the greyscale conversion are all in the path — and the answer is still known, because the trajectory was drawn.
