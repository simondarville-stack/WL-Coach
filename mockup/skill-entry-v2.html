EMOS — Skill-Based Entry · prototype v2 — WYSIWYG stacked input

The input is the stacked notation: comparator symbol in front of the load (click to cycle ≥ ≈ ≤), rep range below the rule. Time constraints are chips that activate when clicked (⏱ total · rest). Works for any exercise incl. combos — it's an input mode, orthogonal to kg/%.

Load and reps are edited directly in the notation (click a value). Reps accept a range (15-25); combo reps keep the tuple, with an optional round-range via the existing multiplier grammar: 4-6(2+1) = 4–6 rounds of 2+1. The + adds a second segment (column). Dashed chips are inactive constraints — click ⏱ or rest to switch them on; ✕ switches off. Ghost chip (≈, coach-only) as in v1: auto from range midpoint & anchor fraction, edit to override, ↺ back to auto.
