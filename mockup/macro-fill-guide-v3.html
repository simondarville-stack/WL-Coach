When applied to a shorter / longer cycle
· Phases & anchor weeks scale proportionally (fractions of length)
· Week-type rhythm: choose repeat block (keep the m/h/h/g cadence, truncate the tail) or stretch (scale the sequence) — repeat is the default
· All values re-interpolate through the same fill engine — a template is just saved fill-guide inputs
Sandbox check: add or remove a week type in the settings mock above — the week-type rhythm multipliers, the table chips, the graph bands and the stamp validation all re-derive from the coach's list. A preset that stamps types the coach doesn't have gets its stamp disabled with a warning instead of failing. Avg mirror: avg is generated at max − 20 % (configurable) and stays linked; dragging an avg point in the graph detaches that week (shown amber in the table). Scope guard: the fill guide works one target at a time by design; only the graph's link-drag touches multiple exercises simultaneously.
